Privacy policy

workstation mail is a personal tool. Its only user is the owner of this site.

  • It reads and sends mail of the owner's own Gmail account only, with the Gmail scopes gmail.readonly and gmail.compose.
  • Its access token stays on the owner's private server. It is never shared, sold, or given to anyone.
  • It sends a reply only after the owner approves that exact reply.
  • It uses no advertising and no analytics, and it keeps no data about other people beyond what the owner's own mailbox holds.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

To end the access: myaccount.google.com/permissions → workstation mail → Remove access.
